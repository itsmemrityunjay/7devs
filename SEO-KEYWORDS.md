How to check: open an **Incognito window**, search the phrase, and note where 7devs.in appears (page 1 = positions 1-10). Better: Google Search Console > Performance, which shows real positions once the site is verified. Re-check monthly. New pages usually take weeks to months to rank.

## How to read the result
- Positions 1-3: strong. 4-10: close, improve the page. 11-30: needs more content and backlinks. Not in top 50: new or too competitive; target a more specific phrase first (for example a city plus service).
- Start with city plus service phrases. They are far less competitive than "digital marketing agency in india".
